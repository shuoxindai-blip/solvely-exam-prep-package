# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students preparing for the Digital SAT who need to move quickly between focused topic learning, a full-length practice test, and targeted improvement. Their primary task is to understand what to study next and start the right learning or practice activity with minimal navigation.

## Product Purpose

The SAT exam prep package brings 100 topic-based lessons, practice questions, a full-length practice test, score reporting, and adaptive improvement into one coherent workflow. Success means students can see the available course content and test status immediately, resume work without ambiguity, and turn practice-test results into focused topic practice.

## Brand Personality

Focused, trustworthy, motivating. The interface should feel like a calm study workspace with clear momentum, not a marketing page or a data-heavy analytics dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Dense grids of equally weighted cards that compete for attention.
- Repeated titles, subtitles, metadata, or technical data-model details that do not help a student choose an action.
- Nested containers and decorative framing that make the course feel fragmented.
- Sample content that appears interactive when it is intentionally unavailable.
- Overly playful, dark, or visually loud styling that departs from the existing Solvely web product.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next study action obvious.
2. Keep Lessons primary and the Practice Test persistently visible as a secondary path.
3. Use progressive disclosure for large topic sets and detailed explanations.
4. Show state and progress only when they change the user's decision.
5. Preserve one consistent Solvely interaction language across learning, testing, and results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All actions and tooltips must be keyboard accessible, focus states must remain visible, status must not rely on color alone, text contrast must remain legible in light and dark themes, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
